# Niom's Restaurant - Dialogflow Training Phrases

Default Welcome Intent
======================
Text response:
    Hello! Welcome to Niom's Restaurant. How can I help you? You can say "New Order" or "Track Order".
    Good day! Welcome to Niom's Restaurant. What can I do for you? You can say "New Order" or "Track Order".
    Greetings! Welcome to Niom's Restaurant. How can I assist you? You can say "New Order" or "Track Order".


Default Fallback Intent
========================
Text Response:
    I didn't understand. You can say 'New Order' or 'Track Order'. In a new order, please mention only items from our menu:
    Chicken Biryani, Murug Polaw, Beef Biryani, Mutton Biryani, Kacchi, Afgani Polaw, Rice, Beef, Mutton Curry, Ilish Fish, and Chingri Curry.
    Also specify a quantity for each item — for example: "1 Kacchi and 2 Chicken Biryani".
